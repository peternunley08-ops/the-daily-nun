Following a story

An editorial illustration of one possible route to a reader.

A story shows up in your feed and feels like it simply arrived. It did not. Someone chose to report it, someone chose to publish it, and someone chose to show it to you.

The first series follows those three choices. It looks past the big claims, checks the evidence, and says so when the evidence changes the answer.
