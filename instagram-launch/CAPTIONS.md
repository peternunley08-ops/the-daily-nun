## Carousel: Who picked this for you?

1. Who picked this for you?
2. Making a story. Recommending it. — A newsroom can report it. A platform can put it in your feed.
3. Trace both. — Find the original reporting. Then examine how it reached you.

Who makes a story and who brings it to your screen can be different. Start with the original reporting, then look at the route it took to reach you. YouTube's 2021 company explanation describes its recommendations; it is not an independent audit of every feed today.

## Carousel: 90% of what?

1. 90% of what?
2. Read the correction. — A 2012 article already flagged outdated ownership details in the infographic it repeated.
3. Check it. Then quote it. — Which market? Which measure? Which year? Who is the source?

A percentage needs a definition. The 2012 Business Insider article repeated an older media-ownership infographic and already flagged outdated ownership information. That does not settle whether media markets are concentrated. It means a current claim needs a clear market, measure, date, and source.

Source: Ashley Lutz, Business Insider, 2012.
https://www.businessinsider.com/these-6-corporations-control-90-of-the-media-in-america-2012-6
