## Editorial standard

- One idea per post. A cover uses at most seven words; a carousel slide's body uses at most 22.
- Curiosity is the common thread across government, media, culture, and future subjects.
- Use the publication name as the brand. Keep any author credit a plain byline.
- Give an answer or a useful distinction after the hook.
- Put sources and qualifications in a short caption; retain context necessary to avoid misleading readers.
- Distinguish a company explaining its product from an independent audit.
- No invented readership, fake engagement, conspiracy claims, or promises of virality.

## Handoff

Editorial brief → source check → final copy → visual/video export → caption and alt-text check → publishing packet. Any new factual claim returns to the source check.
