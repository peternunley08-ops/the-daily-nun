# The Daily Nun — Instagram launch

Open index.html to preview the pack and copy captions.

- Three carousels, three slides each, in carousels/.
- Three 15-second silent text MP4s and optional voiceover scripts in reels/.
- Four optional Story cards in stories/.
- Profile image and bio in brand/.
- Opening newspaper article in article.html.
- Source URLs and accessibility descriptions in content.json and the per-post files.
- Departments and relative posting order in DEPARTMENTS.md.

Upload carousel images in filename order and apply the supplied per-slide alt text when uploading. PNG files do not automatically populate Instagram's accessibility fields. Reel captions include the on-screen transcript; retain it when publishing.

The complete downloadable archive is ../daily-nun-instagram-launch.zip.

All assets are unpublished. No account, handle, audience result, or public destination is claimed. The design and copy are ready for Peter's review.
