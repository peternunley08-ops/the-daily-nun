## Verified Sources

### Forms of Municipal Government
- **Author / Publisher:** National League of Cities (NLC Research & Information)
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** [https://www.nlc.org/resource/forms-of-municipal-government/](https://www.nlc.org/resource/forms-of-municipal-government/)
- **Type:** Civic research & governance guide
- **Analytical Limits:** Describes council-manager and mayor-council structures across U.S. cities. Specific charter powers vary by municipality.

### Procurement Best Practices and Transparency in Public Contracting
- **Author / Publisher:** Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA Best Practices)
- **Date:** 2023
- **URL:** [https://www.gfoa.org/materials/procurement-best-practices](https://www.gfoa.org/materials/procurement-best-practices)
- **Type:** Municipal finance standard
- **Analytical Limits:** Outlines recommended advance public notice requirements and competitive bidding thresholds for local government expenditures.

### Open Government Guide: Access to Public Meetings and Records by State
- **Author / Publisher:** Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press (RCFP)
- **Date:** 2024
- **URL:** [https://www.rcfp.org/open-government-guide/](https://www.rcfp.org/open-government-guide/)
- **Type:** Statutory legal guide
- **Analytical Limits:** Compares state-level open meetings acts (e.g. 72-hour notice requirements and public agenda mandates).
