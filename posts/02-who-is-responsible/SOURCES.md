## Sources

### Census Bureau Releases 2022 Census of Governments: Organization Data
- **Author / Publisher:** U.S. Census Bureau (Census Bureau press release)
- **Date:** August 24, 2023
- **URL:** [https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023/census-of-governments.html](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023/census-of-governments.html)
- **Type:** Federal statistical release
- **Limits:** Counts special districts and independent school districts as governments. It does not describe any one city's charter or who proposes its budget.

### Open Government Guide: California
- **Author / Publisher:** Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press (RCFP)
- **Date:** Checked November 15, 2026
- **URL:** [https://www.rcfp.org/open-government-guide/california/](https://www.rcfp.org/open-government-guide/california/)
- **Type:** Legal guide to a state statute
- **Limits:** Summarizes California's Brown Act: agenda posted at least 72 hours before a regular meeting, and public comment. A secondary summary, and California only. Notice periods differ in other states.

### Florida Statutes, section 286.011
- **Author / Publisher:** State of Florida (Florida Senate, Online Sunshine)
- **Date:** 2025 statutes
- **URL:** [https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/286.011](https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2025/286.011)
- **Type:** State statute
- **Limits:** Requires 'reasonable notice' of meetings and recorded minutes. It sets no fixed number of hours.
