# Voiceover & Video Script: Who signs the check?

**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**Format:** Vertical Video (1080 × 1920) / Reel & TikTok

## Voiceover Script (For Peter to record)
"When people get frustrated by a local decision, they usually tag the mayor. But in local government, authority is legally divided. Mayors propose budgets, but city councils vote on them. School boards award their own contracts. And transit runs on separate boards. The real question isn't who has the biggest microphone—it's who has the legal authority to sign the check. The Daily Nun."

## Kinetic On-Screen Text
1. Angry about a local policy?
2. Don't just tag the mayor.
3. Authority is legally divided.
4. Mayors propose. Councils vote.
5. School boards contract separately.
6. Before a dollar moves, it must appear on a public meeting agenda.
7. The minutes show who voted yes.
8. The Daily Nun / A closer look at the world.

## Delivery Notes
- Tone: Calm, authoritative, third-person analytical.
- Pacing: Measured, clear enunciation, ~140 words per minute.
- Video functions silently with on-screen kinetic typography.
