# Source notes — Week 3 Culture Desk

**Title:** When does a recommendation become manipulation?
**Desk:** Culture & Attention
**Status:** Prepared draft / Unpublished

## Verified Sources

### Online Manipulation: Hidden Influences in a Digital World
- **Author / Publisher:** Daniel Susser, Beate Roessler, Helen Nissenbaum (Georgetown Law Technology Review, 4 Geo. L. Tech. Rev. 1)
- **Date:** January 2020 (volume dated 2019)
- **URL:** [https://georgetownlawtechreview.org/online-manipulation-hidden-influences-in-a-digital-world/GLTR-01-2020/](https://georgetownlawtechreview.org/online-manipulation-hidden-influences-in-a-digital-world/GLTR-01-2020/)
- **Type:** Law review article; interpretation, not an empirical study
- **Analytical Limits:** One scholarly definition among several. The authors note that not everyone agrees hiddenness is required for manipulation. Definition passages read from the published PDF on 2026-11-13.

### How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou
- **Author / Publisher:** TikTok (TikTok Newsroom)
- **Date:** June 18, 2020
- **URL:** [https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **Type:** Corporate statement
- **Analytical Limits:** The company's own description from 2020. It is not independently audited and may not describe the current system.

### Instagram Ranking Explained
- **Author / Publisher:** Adam Mosseri (Instagram)
- **Date:** May 31, 2023
- **URL:** [https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)
- **Type:** Corporate statement
- **Analytical Limits:** The company's own description from 2023. It lists signals and predictions but not their weights, and it is not independently audited.

### On YouTube's recommendation system
- **Author / Publisher:** Cristos Goodrow (YouTube Official Blog)
- **Date:** September 15, 2021
- **URL:** [https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/)
- **Type:** Corporate statement
- **Analytical Limits:** The company's own description from 2021. It says recommendations drive 'a significant amount' of viewership and gives no figure.

### Engagement, user satisfaction, and the amplification of divisive content on social media
- **Author / Publisher:** Smitha Milli, Micah Carroll, Yike Wang, et al. (PNAS Nexus 4(3), pgaf062)
- **Date:** March 5, 2025
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf062](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf062)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed preregistered algorithmic audit
- **Analytical Limits:** 806 U.S. Twitter users recruited through a crowd-work platform, February 11-27, 2023. Sample skewed younger and more Democratic. One platform at one point in time; no long-term effects measured; only the first 10 tweets per timeline were surveyed.

### How do social media feed algorithms affect attitudes and behavior in an election campaign?
- **Author / Publisher:** Andrew M. Guess, et al. (Science)
- **Date:** July 27, 2023
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp9364](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp9364)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed randomized field experiment
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, verified through Europe PMC for the repository's media-democracy report (source E01); the publisher page returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-13. Consenting Facebook and Instagram users over three months in 2020; not an estimate of all accumulated platform effects.

### A Look Behind the Screens: Examining the Data Practices of Social Media and Video Streaming Services (press release)
- **Author / Publisher:** Federal Trade Commission staff (Federal Trade Commission)
- **Date:** September 19, 2024
- **URL:** [https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-staff-report-finds-large-social-media-video-streaming-companies-have-engaged-vast-surveillance](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/09/ftc-staff-report-finds-large-social-media-video-streaming-companies-have-engaged-vast-surveillance)
- **Type:** Government staff report (press release read; full report not read)
- **Analytical Limits:** Based on orders issued in December 2020 to nine companies. Concerns data practices, not the effects of ranking on beliefs or purchases.

### For shopping, phones are common and influencers have become a factor – especially for young adults
- **Author / Publisher:** Michelle Faverio and Monica Anderson (Pew Research Center)
- **Date:** November 21, 2022
- **URL:** [https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2022/11/21/for-shopping-phones-are-common-and-influencers-have-become-a-factor-especially-for-young-adults/](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2022/11/21/for-shopping-phones-are-common-and-influencers-have-become-a-factor-especially-for-young-adults/)
- **Type:** Survey research
- **Analytical Limits:** 6,034 U.S. adults, July 5-17, 2022. Denominator for the purchase figures is adult social media users. Self-reported; does not establish that posts caused purchases. No respondents under 18.

### Psychological targeting as an effective approach to digital mass persuasion
- **Author / Publisher:** Sandra C. Matz, Michal Kosinski, Gideon Nave, David J. Stillwell (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences)
- **Date:** November 13, 2017
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1710966114](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1710966114)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed field experiments
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, read through Europe PMC on 2026-11-13. Three experiments reaching over 3.5 million people. 'Up to' figures are best cases, not averages. Tests tailored ads, not feed ranking.

### America's News Influencers
- **Author / Publisher:** Pew Research Center (Pew Research Center)
- **Date:** November 18, 2024
- **URL:** [https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/2024/11/18/americas-news-influencers/](https://www.pewresearch.org/journalism/2024/11/18/americas-news-influencers/)
- **Type:** Survey research
- **Analytical Limits:** 10,658 U.S. adults, July 15-August 4, 2024. Measures where people say they regularly get news, not whether it changed their views. No respondents under 18.

### The small effects of political advertising are small regardless of context, message, sender, or receiver: Evidence from 59 real-time randomized experiments
- **Author / Publisher:** Alexander Coppock, Seth J. Hill, Lynn Vavreck (Science Advances)
- **Date:** September 2, 2020
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abc4046](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abc4046)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed randomized experiments
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, read through Europe PMC on 2026-11-13. 49 ads, 59 experiments, 34,000 people, 2016 U.S. presidential campaign. Survey experiments on ad exposure, not feed ranking.

### A 2 million-person, campaign-wide field experiment shows how digital advertising affects voter turnout
- **Author / Publisher:** Minali Aggarwal, Jennifer Allen, Alexander Coppock, et al. (Nature Human Behaviour)
- **Date:** January 12, 2023
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01487-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-022-01487-4)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed field experiment
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, read through Europe PMC on 2026-11-13. 2 million voters in five battleground states, 2020. Measures turnout, not trust. One partisan ad program.

### The effects of Facebook and Instagram on the 2020 election: A deactivation experiment
- **Author / Publisher:** Hunt Allcott, Matthew Gentzkow, Winter Mason, et al. (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences)
- **Date:** May 13, 2024
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2321584121](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2321584121)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed randomized experiment
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, read through Europe PMC on 2026-11-13. A randomized subset of 19,857 Facebook and 15,585 Instagram users deactivated for six weeks before the 2020 election. Tests access to the platforms, not the ranking system alone.

### Out-group animosity drives engagement on social media
- **Author / Publisher:** Steve Rathje, Jay J. Van Bavel, Sander van der Linden (Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences)
- **Date:** June 2021
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2024292118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2024292118)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed observational research
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract only, read through Europe PMC on 2026-11-13 (also source M03 in the media-democracy report). 2,730,215 posts from news media and U.S. congressional accounts on Facebook and Twitter. Associations between wording and shares; does not show that posts made readers angrier or generated profit.
