# Voiceover & Video Script: Who chose that video?

**Duration:** 30 seconds (voiceover: 69 words, about 30 seconds at 140 words per minute)
**Format:** Vertical Video (1080 × 1920) / Reel & TikTok

## Voiceover Script (For Peter to record)
"Every feed is a ranking. TikTok, Instagram and YouTube say their systems predict what each person will watch, like or share. In 2023, researchers audited 806 U.S. Twitter users. Ranking by engagement showed them more angry, hostile posts than a newest-first feed, and they rated its political picks lower. One study is not the whole story. But a click is not the same as wanting more. The Daily Nun."

## Kinetic On-Screen Text
1. Who chose that video?
2. A feed is a ranking.
3. Platforms say they predict what gets watched, liked, shared.
4. 2023: researchers audited 806 U.S. Twitter users.
5. Engagement ranking showed more angry, hostile posts.
6. Users rated its political picks lower.
7. One study. Not the whole story.
8. A click is not approval.
9. The Daily Nun / A closer look at the world.

## Delivery Notes
- Tone: Calm, authoritative, third-person analytical.
- Pacing: Measured, clear enunciation, about 140 words per minute.
- Video works silently with on-screen kinetic typography.
