Slide 1: What happens when the local paper closes? Subtitle: Fewer reporters cover local government. Researchers have measured some of what changes.

Slide 2: 238 counties, no local outlet. A 2026 Northwestern count found 238 U.S. counties with no locally based professional news source, and over 1,400 with only one.

Slide 3: Closures can cost money. One study estimated that after a local paper closed, the town's borrowing costs rose about 650,000 dollars per bond issue.
