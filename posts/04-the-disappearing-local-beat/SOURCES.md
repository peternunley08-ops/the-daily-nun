## Verified Sources

### The State of Local News 2026
- **Author / Publisher:** Zach Metzger and John Volk (Medill Local News Initiative, Northwestern University)
- **Date:** October 13, 2026
- **URL:** [https://localnewsinitiative.northwestern.edu/projects/state-of-local-news/2026/report/](https://localnewsinitiative.northwestern.edu/projects/state-of-local-news/2026/report/)
- **Type:** Original research inventory
- **Analytical Limits:** Counts locally based professional outlets, not every information source. The 170 figure combines closures and mergers. Methodology changed this year. Figures from the repository's report verification of 2026-11-11 (source E08); the site returned HTTP 503 and a certificate error on 2026-11-15.

### Financing dies in darkness? The impact of newspaper closures on public finance
- **Author / Publisher:** Pengjie Gao, Chang Lee, Dermot Murphy (Journal of Financial Economics 135(2), 445-467)
- **Date:** 2020
- **URL:** [https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v135y2020i2p445-467.html](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v135y2020i2p445-467.html)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed research
- **Analytical Limits:** Publisher abstract read on RePEc on 2026-11-15; full data, sample period, and methods not reviewed. The 5-11 basis point and $650,000 figures and the causal claim are the authors' own.

### Press Coverage and Political Accountability
- **Author / Publisher:** James M. Snyder Jr., David Strömberg (NBER Working Paper 13878; Journal of Political Economy (2010))
- **Date:** March 2008
- **URL:** [https://www.nber.org/papers/w13878](https://www.nber.org/papers/w13878)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed research (working paper version read)
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract read on 2026-11-15. Concerns U.S. House coverage in earlier decades, not newspaper closures directly.

### Local News and National Politics
- **Author / Publisher:** Gregory J. Martin, Joshua McCrain (American Political Science Review)
- **Date:** February 19, 2019
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055418000965](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055418000965)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed research
- **Analytical Limits:** Abstract read through Crossref on 2026-11-15. Concerns one conglomerate's acquisitions of local TV stations; results are relative to other programs in the same markets.
