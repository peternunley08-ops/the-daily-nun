Fewer reporters cover local government. The Daily Nun looked at what researchers have measured when the local paper closes.

A 2026 Northwestern count found 238 U.S. counties with no locally based professional news source. Studies link closures and thinner coverage to higher public borrowing costs and less-informed voters. They show patterns, not what happens in any one town.
