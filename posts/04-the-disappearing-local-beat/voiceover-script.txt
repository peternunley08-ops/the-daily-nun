# Voiceover & Video Script: Who is watching the meeting?

**Duration:** ~30 seconds
**Format:** Vertical Video (1080 × 1920) / Reel & TikTok

## Voiceover Script (For Peter to record)
"What happens when the local paper closes? A 2026 Northwestern count found 238 U.S. counties with no locally based professional news source. One study estimated that after a local paper closed, the town's borrowing costs went up, about six hundred fifty thousand dollars per bond issue. Another found voters with less coverage were less likely to know their representative's name. The meeting still happens. Who is watching? The Daily Nun."
