Slide 1: Who decides who gets the hours? Subtitle: How work schedules get set, and what California law says when a shift disappears.

Slide 2: Short notice is common. A 2019 survey of 30,000 workers at large chains found two-thirds got less than two weeks' notice of their schedules.

Slide 3: Know the half-shift rule. In California, workers sent home with under half their shift done are generally owed half the shift's pay, two to four hours.
