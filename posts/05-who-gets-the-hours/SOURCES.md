## Sources

### It's About Time: How Work Schedule Instability Matters for Workers, Families, and Racial Inequality
- **Author / Publisher:** Daniel Schneider and Kristen Harknett (The Shift Project, Harvard Kennedy School)
- **Date:** October 16, 2019
- **URL:** [https://shift.hks.harvard.edu/its-about-time-how-work-schedule-instability-matters-for-workers-families-and-racial-inequality/](https://shift.hks.harvard.edu/its-about-time-how-work-schedule-instability-matters-for-workers-families-and-racial-inequality/)
- **Type:** Survey research brief
- **Limits:** About 30,000 employees at 120 of the largest U.S. retail and food-service firms; very large employers only; the brief does not break out high-school or student workers, and small businesses are not covered. Hardship findings are associations, not proof that schedules caused them. Read in full on 2026-11-15.

### Reporting time pay: frequently asked questions
- **Author / Publisher:** California Labor Commissioner's Office (DLSE) (California Department of Industrial Relations)
- **Date:** Undated web page, read 2026-11-15
- **URL:** [https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_reportingtimepay.htm](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/faq_reportingtimepay.htm)
- **Type:** Official state guidance
- **Limits:** Guidance, not the regulation itself (IWC wage orders, section 5). Has exceptions: threats, utility failures, acts of God, paid standby, shifts under two hours, and workers who leave on their own.

### Complying With Child Labor Laws: Information for Employers
- **Author / Publisher:** California Labor Commissioner's Office (DLSE) (California Department of Industrial Relations)
- **Date:** April 2026 (PDF creation date)
- **URL:** [https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/Complying-with-Child-Labor-Laws-English.pdf](https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/Complying-with-Child-Labor-Laws-English.pdf)
- **Type:** Official state fact sheet
- **Limits:** Summary for employers. Exceptions exist, such as school-approved work experience programs. The statute (Labor Code section 1391) was not opened; the legislature's site blocked automated access.

### Fair Work Week Information
- **Author / Publisher:** City of Los Angeles, Office of Wage Standards (City of Los Angeles)
- **Date:** Ordinance effective April 1, 2023; page read 2026-11-15
- **URL:** [https://wagesla.lacity.gov/fair-work-week-information](https://wagesla.lacity.gov/fair-work-week-information)
- **Type:** Official city guidance
- **Limits:** Applies only to retail employers with 300 or more employees globally, for work inside the City of Los Angeles. Other cities' rules were not checked.
