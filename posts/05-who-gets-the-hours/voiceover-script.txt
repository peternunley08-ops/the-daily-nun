# Voiceover & Video Script: Who gets the hours?

**Duration:** 30 seconds (voiceover: 63 words, about 27 seconds at 140 words per minute)
**Format:** Vertical Video (1080 × 1920) / Reel & TikTok

## Voiceover Script (For Peter to record)
"Who decides who gets the hours? The employer does. A 2019 survey of thirty thousand workers at big chains found two in three got less than two weeks' notice. California sets a few limits. Sent home with under half a shift done? The worker is generally owed half the shift's pay. Under eighteen? Four hours, max, on a school day. The Daily Nun."

## Kinetic On-Screen Text
1. Who decides who gets the hours?
2. The employer does.
3. 2019: 30,000 workers at big chains surveyed.
4. Two in three: under two weeks' notice.
5. California sets a few limits.
6. Sent home early? Some pay is owed.
7. Under 18? Four hours on school days.
8. The Daily Nun / A closer look at the world.

## Delivery Notes
- Tone: Calm, authoritative, third-person analytical.
- Pacing: Measured, clear enunciation, about 140 words per minute.
- Video works silently with on-screen kinetic typography.
