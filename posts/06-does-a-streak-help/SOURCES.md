## Sources

### The Duolingo Streak Uses Habit Research to Keep You Motivated
- **Author / Publisher:** Osman Mansur (Duolingo Blog)
- **Date:** January 31, 2022
- **URL:** [https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)
- **Type:** Corporate statement
- **Limits:** The company describing its own design and reporting its own A/B test results (+1.7% seven-day retention from milestone animations; +0.38% relative daily active learners from two streak freezes). Not independently verified. Read in full on 2026-11-15.

### Improving the streak: Forming habits one lesson at a time
- **Author / Publisher:** Anton Yu (Duolingo Blog)
- **Date:** November 19, 2020
- **URL:** [https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/)
- **Type:** Corporate statement
- **Limits:** Company-reported result (over 40% more learners on a 7+ day streak after decoupling streaks from daily goals). Measures streak counts, not learning. Not independently verified.

### How are habits formed: Modelling habit formation in the real world
- **Author / Publisher:** Phillippa Lally, Cornelia H. M. van Jaarsveld, Henry W. W. Potts, Jane Wardle (European Journal of Social Psychology 40(6), 998-1009)
- **Date:** Published online July 16, 2009 (print October 2010)
- **URL:** [https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed study
- **Limits:** Abstract only, read through Crossref on 2026-11-15. 96 volunteers, 82 analyzed, 39 with good model fit; small sample. Studied eating, drinking, or exercise habits, not app streaks or learning.

### On or Off Track: How (Broken) Streaks Affect Consumer Decisions
- **Author / Publisher:** Jackie Silverman, Alixandra Barasch (Journal of Consumer Research 49(6), 1095-1117)
- **Date:** April 2023
- **URL:** [https://www.colorado.edu/business/faculty-research/2023/04/19/or-track-how-broken-streaks-affect-consumer-decisions](https://www.colorado.edu/business/faculty-research/2023/04/19/or-track-how-broken-streaks-affect-consumer-decisions)
- **Type:** Peer-reviewed research (seven studies)
- **Limits:** Abstract only, read on the University of Colorado's publication page on 2026-11-15. Measures subsequent engagement, not learning outcomes. Effect sizes and samples not read.
