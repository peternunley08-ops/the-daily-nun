A streak counts days. The Daily Nun looked at what it is built to do.

Duolingo says its streak taps "loss aversion" once the novelty fades. Research finds a missed day barely affects a habit, while seeing a streak reset can lead people to do less. The Daily Nun found no study showing that streaks improve learning.
